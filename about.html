About Code Connect

Our mission is to engage students with coding and technology education.

Our Mission

Code Connect aims to provide students with the resources and support they need to learn coding and technology skills. We believe that every student should have the opportunity to explore the world of technology and develop the skills necessary for the future.

We organize workshops, hackathons, and seminars to engage students and provide hands-on learning experiences. Our community is built on collaboration, support, and a shared passion for technology.
